Random video chat safety tips

A safer random chat starts with simple boundaries and fast action when something feels wrong.

Keep private details private

Do not share your full name, address, school, workplace, phone number, financial details, or account passwords. Random chat should stay casual.

Use skip early

If someone pressures you, asks for offsite contact too quickly, or makes you uncomfortable, use Next. You do not owe anyone more time.

Report rule breaking

Reports give moderators context and help remove abusive users faster. Use the in-chat Report button for behavior problems, not the general feedback form.
