Matching

Use interests to match faster

Interests help the matchmaker understand what kind of conversation you want.

Use 3 to 5 focused interests

Specific topics usually work better than broad ones. "Minecraft", "anime", "gym", or "Taylor Swift" gives stronger matching signal than "fun" or "chat".

Mix broad and specific

A good set might include one broad category and a few specific topics. For example: music, guitar, hip hop, concerts.

Update when the queue feels slow

If matches are taking longer than expected, remove very narrow tags or add one popular interest. You can always skip and change topics later.
